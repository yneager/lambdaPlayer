Download **LAMBDA-Player-Setup-0.2.9-x64.exe** and run it, or click **Update** in an updater-enabled LAMBDA installation. The installer includes the complete Windows x64 app and dependencies; the ZIP is the portable app and in-app update runtime.

- Fixed fast-moving scenes being mistaken for cuts and holding original frames. Wider motion matching preserves interpolation during fast pans. Normal RIFE x2/60 no longer adapt their output target down to the original cadence.
- Improved scene-switch protection with whole-shot color comparison and dedicated checks for dark, low-contrast cuts. Detected cuts hold the preceding original until the next source timestamp instead of warping between shots.
- FPS now counts newly presented video pictures at completed window swaps, excluding redraws and held originals. Moved the former interpolation icon to FPS and added a new interpolation icon.
- Aligned folder cards under **Your library** with the other card rows, including the shared padding at narrow window sizes.
- Reset OpenGL upload state before video rendering to protect against texture corruption when subtitle textures are added. Selecting subtitles on NVIDIA direct-sharing decoders switches to copy-based hardware decoding while preserving source resolution and bit depth.
- Check for updates quietly on every launch. A cached newer update can appear immediately; **Later** snoozes it until the next launch. Offline checks remain quiet.

Validation: release build and Qt regressions passed, the OpenGL transfer test passed on the local GPU, and 4K fast-motion and scene-cut checks passed at 48, 60 and 240 FPS. The subtitle safeguard still needs confirmation on the reported RTX 5080; it has not been tested on that GPU locally. Interpolation may still produce artifacts in challenging footage—please report a sample and the selected mode.
