LAMBDA Player 0.2.8 (Windows 10/11 x64)

Run the LAMBDA-Player-Setup-<version>-x64.exe file. It installs for your Windows user,
with no separate Qt, Python, mpv, or streaming engine installation needed.
Alternatively, open "LAMBDA Player\LambdaPlayer.exe" directly.

Home > Your library > Add folder:
Choose one movie or one series folder. Season subfolders are scanned too.
Click its card to see/play the files. Find artwork lets you correct a title
match; Rescan folder adds newly downloaded episodes. Removing a card does
not delete videos. Posters are cached for offline use after matching.

Playback position is saved every five seconds, on pause, when returning
Home, when switching videos, and on normal exit. Continue Watching includes
local and online episodes. Online entries reopen their saved episode's
sources; choose an available stream, and playback resumes at the saved time.
Online catalogs and sources depend on the add-ons installed on your PC.
Finished videos play again from the beginning.

To make it the default video player:
Windows Settings > Apps > Default apps > LAMBDA Player. Choose the file
types you want. Or right-click a video > Open with > Choose another app >
LAMBDA Player > Always. Setup offers the application without replacing
your existing defaults automatically.

Please test: nested season folders, correct/wrong artwork matches, resume
after restarting, opening a file from Explorer, online episode resume,
subtitles, fullscreen, scrolling, interpolation, and uninstalling.
Interpolation throughput depends on your GPU and display refresh rate.

This build is intended for testing and feedback.

ONE-CLICK UPDATES
In an updater-enabled build, click Update inside LAMBDA. It downloads and verifies
the runtime, then restarts automatically. Your library and playback positions stay
saved. A failed startup restores the previous version. GitHub hosts files invisibly;
you do not need an account or to visit a website.
Older builds without this updater require one installation of this build first.

PLAYER CONTROLS
Click FPS in the player controls to show/hide the FPS overlay. Qt swaps measure
presentation activity, including repeated frames; source/filter/target rates
are labeled separately. Choose Smoothness > Toggle interpolation shortcut to
record a shortcut with Ctrl or Alt. Clear and save to disable it. Toggling on
restores the last chosen available interpolation mode.

Click Install default addons beside Add-ons to install Cinemeta, OpenSubtitles
v3 and Torrentio. Existing configurations are preserved. If all are present,
the button says All installed. Removing an addon or a failed download makes
it possible to install only the missing addon on the next click.

INTERPOLATION AND ADDON FIXES
The top-menu default-addon button stays visible, including when it says All
installed. Universal modes now use reusable neural flow instead of coarse
block matching. RIFE x2/60 keep their existing motion settings. Scene-cut
checks include spatial correspondence for shots with similar colors; genuine
cuts hold the preceding original frame until the next source timestamp.
Please retest the movies and exact scene switches that showed artifacts.
