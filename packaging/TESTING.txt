LAMBDA Player - friend test build (Windows 10/11 x64)

Run LAMBDA-Player-Setup-0.2.7-test.1-x64.exe. It installs for your Windows user,
with no separate Qt, Python, mpv, or streaming engine installation needed.
Alternatively, open "LAMBDA Player\LambdaPlayer.exe" directly.

Home > Your library > Add folder:
Choose one movie or one series folder. Season subfolders are scanned too.
Click its card to see/play the files. Find artwork lets you correct a title
match; Rescan folder adds newly downloaded episodes. Removing a card does
not delete videos. Posters are cached for offline use after matching.

Playback position is saved every five seconds, on pause, when returning
Home, when switching videos, and on normal exit. Continue Watching includes
local and online episodes. Online entries reopen their saved episode's
sources; choose an available stream, and playback resumes at the saved time.
Online catalogs and sources depend on the add-ons installed on your PC.
Finished videos play again from the beginning.

To make it the default video player:
Windows Settings > Apps > Default apps > LAMBDA Player. Choose the file
types you want. Or right-click a video > Open with > Choose another app >
LAMBDA Player > Always. Setup offers the application without replacing
your existing defaults automatically.

Please test: nested season folders, correct/wrong artwork matches, resume
after restarting, opening a file from Explorer, online episode resume,
subtitles, fullscreen, scrolling, interpolation, and uninstalling.
Interpolation throughput depends on your GPU and display refresh rate.

This is the public 0.2.7-test.1 prerelease for testing and feedback.
